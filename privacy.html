Privacy policy

Q rclone Drive · last updated 28 October 2026

Who uses it

Q rclone Drive is a private tool used only by its owner on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner runs it, the tool uses the Google Drive API, with the owner's permission, to upload, list, move, rename and delete files in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it stores and shares

  • Files travel only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.
  • The access token that Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on any other server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from the Google Account page Third-party apps & services.
